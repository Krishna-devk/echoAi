# Design System: AI Resume Analyser (Antigravity Edition)

## Theme: "Obsidian Echo"
A premium, dark-first aesthetic with glassmorphism, glowing accents, and high-performance feel.

### Color Palette (HSL)
- **Background:** `hsl(240, 10%, 4%)` (Deep Obsidian)
- **Surface:** `hsl(240, 10%, 8%)`
- **Surface-Accent:** `hsl(240, 10%, 12%)`
- **Primary:** `hsl(263, 70%, 50%)` (Royal Purple)
- **Primary-Glow:** `hsla(263, 70%, 50%, 0.4)`
- **Secondary:** `hsl(190, 90%, 50%)` (Electric Cyan)
- **Text-Primary:** `hsl(0, 0%, 98%)`
- **Text-Secondary:** `hsl(240, 5%, 65%)`
- **Border:** `hsla(240, 5%, 20%, 0.5)`

### Typography
- **Headings:** *Outfit* or *Inter* (Semi-bold to Bold)
- **Body:** *Inter* (Regular to Medium)
- **Monospace:** *JetBrains Mono* (for code/scores)

### Components (shadcn-ui based)
- **Buttons:** Subtle glow on hover, spring transitions.
- **Cards:** `backdrop-blur-xl` with thin `1px` borders and radial gradients.
- **Charts:** Minimalist Recharts with gradient fills.

### Animations (Framer Motion)
- **Page Transitions:** Staggered fade-ins.
- **Interactions:** Subtle scale-up (1.02) on card hover.
- **Loading:** Pulsing orb animation.
